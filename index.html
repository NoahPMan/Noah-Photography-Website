About

Noah Manaigre

I’m a Winnipeg-based photographer with a passion for sports, portraits, street life, and nature. I’m drawn to moments that feel genuine, whether it’s the intensity of a game, someone’s personality coming through in a portrait, or an unexpected scene that catches my attention.

My approach is relaxed and focused. I pay attention to composition, lighting, and the details that make each moment unique, creating photographs that feel natural, polished, and true to the subject.
