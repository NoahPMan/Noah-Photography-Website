About

Noah Manaigre

I'm a photographer based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, specializing in sports, portrait, street, and nature photography. I'm drawn to real moments and clean compositions that feel natural rather than staged.

Whatever I'm shooting, my goal is the same: sharp, well-composed images that hold up on their own and feel true to the moment they were taken.
